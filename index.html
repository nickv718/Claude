Who this is for, and who it isn't

For you if

  • You're curious about memecoins but don't know how a launch, a liquidity pool or a bonding curve works.
  • You've bought a few tokens on hype and want an actual process for what to check first.
  • You want to keep your wallet safe from drainers, fake airdrops and bad approvals.
  • You'll only put in money you can afford to lose completely.

Not for you if

  • You want signals, "next 100x" calls or someone to tell you what to buy.
  • You're looking for guaranteed income. Nobody can honestly promise that, and most memecoins go to zero.
  • You plan to trade with rent money, savings or borrowed funds.
